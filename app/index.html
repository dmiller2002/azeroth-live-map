      DRUNKS AZEROTH UI · GUILD

      Guild

      Connect WoW to sync your guild roster.

      For professions and precise map pins, guild members need addon 0.8.0 and /daui guild on in WoW. Sharing sends your primary professions and coordinates to your guild. Use /daui guild off to stop.

      Guild members, professions, and locations

      Connect WoW for live guild information.
